About NB Connect

Our Story

NB Connect was founded with a simple mission: to create a secure, reliable, and user-friendly platform that connects buyers and sellers across South Africa. We believe everyone deserves access to a trustworthy marketplace where they can buy and sell goods with confidence.

Our Mission

To empower South African communities by providing a secure and accessible C2C e-commerce platform that fosters trust, convenience, and economic growth.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading C2C marketplace, known for security, reliability, and community-driven commerce.
